# Arlo — Positioning & Brand Rules

The single source of truth for how we talk about Arlo. Website, ads, scripts,
sales calls, and agent config should all stay consistent with this.

## What Arlo is

**Arlo is an AI solutions company.** Voice agents are the flagship product and
the way we get in the door — but Arlo is not "the voice agent company." The
brand is deliberately broad so it can grow into other AI services (chatbots,
automations, lead reactivation, etc.) without rebranding.

- **Company / brand:** Arlo — "AI that works for your business."
- **First product:** an AI phone agent (AI receptionist) that answers every
  call, qualifies it, books it, and summarises it for the owner.
- **Future:** additional AI solutions under the same Arlo brand.

## What "Angel" is (and isn't)

**Angel is Arlo's own in-house agent — not the product we sell.**

- Angel answers Arlo's own line and makes Arlo's outbound demo/sales calls.
  Angel is the mascot / demo — "this is what your customers would experience."
- We do **not** sell "Angel" to clients. When Arlo deploys for a business, the
  agent becomes *that business's* receptionist — greets with their name, uses
  their services/hours, and can be named whatever the owner wants.
- Client agents are **white-labelled to the client**, configured per business.
  Angel is only ever Arlo's own.

## The core rule: brand broad, sell narrow

- **Brand broad:** Arlo the company = AI solutions. Keep the vision open.
- **Sell narrow:** outreach right now is laser-focused on one vertical and one
  painfully concrete promise — e.g. "an AI receptionist for plumbers so you
  never miss a job." Do **not** pitch "we do all AI solutions" to a prospect;
  that sounds like a consultant, not a fix for their missed calls.
- The brand is a house; prospects enter through one specific door first.

## Language do / don't

- ✅ "Arlo is an AI receptionist for your business." / "AI phone agents for UK
  small business." / "AI solutions for [industry]."
- ✅ Per-client: "your receptionist", or the client's chosen name.
- ❌ "Get Angel." / "Angel is our product." / selling Angel by name to clients.
- ❌ Boxing the brand in as only-voice, or pitching broad AI solutions in cold
  outreach.

## Why nothing built so far is wasted

The two Retell agents we built — Angel (inbound receptionist) and Angel
(outbound demo) — are Arlo's **demo and sales layer**. Real clients get their
own separately-configured, white-labelled agents. The templates, prompts, n8n
workflow, and setup script all carry over; only the naming/branding changes
per client.
