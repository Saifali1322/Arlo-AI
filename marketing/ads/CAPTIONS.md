image, paste the caption, done. Swap the CTA once your website/UK number is
live. Since your demo number is currently US, the CTA drives to **DM/comment**
rather than "call this number" for now.

## The content that will actually beat all of these

Static ads build awareness, but your single highest-performing post will be a
**video of you calling Angel live** — phone on speaker, Angel answering
naturally, captioned "This is what your customers hear at 6pm on a Friday when
you miss their call." Film it once, post it everywhere. One clip of the product
sounding uncannily human will outperform any graphic.
