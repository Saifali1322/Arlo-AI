## LinkedIn — build in public (text post; optionally attach `arlo_ad_3_offer.png`)

I'm building an AI receptionist for UK small businesses. Here's where we're at.

The problem is dead simple: most small businesses miss more than half their
calls. Someone's on a job, with a customer, or it's after hours. And most people
who reach voicemail don't ring back — they just call the next name on the list.
Every missed call is a job that quietly went to a competitor.

So we built Arlo: an AI phone agent that answers every call, 24/7, in a natural
British voice. It works out what the caller needs, books them straight into the
calendar, texts them a confirmation, and sends the owner a summary of every
call. You keep your number. You just turn up to the work.

The part I still can't quite believe: this week our own agent made a live call
to a real person. They asked if it was AI (it told them, straight away). They
tested it with a random question. They pushed back with objections. It handled
all of it — and booked a follow-up before the call ended.

We're a small team and it's early days. But it's live, and it works.

If you run a business that lives on the phone — or you know someone who does —
I'd genuinely love to show you what it sounds like. Drop me a comment or a DM.

Building in public. More soon. 👇
