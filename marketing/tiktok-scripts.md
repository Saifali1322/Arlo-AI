# Arlo — 5 TikTok Scripts

All under 45s, phone-shot, hook lands in the first 2 seconds. Film in order —
#1 is the highest-leverage, do it first.

---

## 1. "Call the demo live" (the flagship — do this one first)

**Hook (0-2s):** You, phone to your ear, straight to camera: *"Watch what happens when I call my own business."*

**Action:** Dial +1 608 946 0425 on speaker. Let Angel actually answer and talk for real — don't cut it.

**On-screen text overlay (appears as she answers):** "This is AI. Not a person."

**You, after ~10-15 sec of real call audio, cut back to camera:** *"That's Angel. She answers every call for my company, 24/7, so we never miss a job."*

**Caption:** This is what your customers hear at 6pm on a Friday when you miss their call. #smallbusinessuk #ai #buildinpublic #tradesuk

**Why it works:** Zero acting, zero script for Angel — the real product IS the content. Proof beats claims every time.

---

## 2. POV: the missed call

**Hook (0-2s):** On-screen text: "POV: you're under a sink and your phone's ringing" — show yourself mid-task (fixing something, up a ladder, whatever you can stage), phone visibly buzzing just out of reach.

**Beat 1 (2-6s):** You, straight to camera, slightly out of breath: *"That call? Gone. They won't ring back — they'll just call the next name on Google."*

**Beat 2 (6-15s):** Cut to phone screen recording of a real Angel call transcript or the calendar event she booked. Voiceover: *"Unless someone else answers it for you. Every call, 24/7, booked straight into your calendar."*

**On-screen text (final 2s):** "Comment ARLO to hear it."

**Caption:** You didn't lose the job because you're bad at it. #tradesuk #plumberlife #smallbusinessuk

**Why it works:** Dramatizes the exact pain your ad campaign already uses — visual proof of the moment money is lost.

---

## 3. Myth-bust / reveal

**Hook (0-2s):** Straight to camera, deadpan: *"I called a business today and a robot answered the phone. It was not what I expected."*

**Beat (2-20s):** Play 10-15 sec of a real Angel call clip — ideally a moment where she handles an interruption or an odd question naturally.

**You, reacting:** *"I kept trying to catch her out. Couldn't do it. She sounds more human than half the call centres I've actually spoken to."*

**On-screen text:** "Her name's Angel. She works for Arlo."

**Caption:** Nobody believes me until they hear it. #ai #voiceai #buildinpublic

**Why it works:** "I tried to break it and couldn't" is one of the strongest social-proof hooks in AI content right now — invites the viewer to try it themselves.

---

## 4. Challenge / comment-bait

**Hook (0-2s):** *"Comment ANYTHING and I'll actually call this AI and ask her live."*

**Beat:** Screen-record yourself scrolling to a comment, then calling Angel and asking that exact question on camera, unscripted.

**On-screen text overlay:** "Testing: [the actual question]"

**End card:** *"Drop another one — I'll do this every day this week."*

**Caption:** Ask my AI receptionist anything. First one's from @[commenter]. #ai #interactive #buildinpublic

**Why it works:** Turns your page into a running series (repeatable format = algorithm loves it), and every comment is free R&D on how Angel handles curveballs.

---

## 5. Founder / build-in-public numbers

**Hook (0-2s):** Screen recording of your Retell call log or n8n dashboard, on-screen text: *"My AI made its first sale by itself this week."*

**Beat (2-20s):** You, talking over a quick screen-recording montage (call log → transcript snippet → calendar event appearing): *"It called someone, got asked if it was AI, got tested with a random question, handled an objection — and booked a follow-up. I didn't say a word."*

**On-screen text (final beat):** "Small business. Big phone bills saved."

**Caption:** Building an AI that sells itself — literally, down the phone. Day [X] of building Arlo. #buildinpublic #startup #ai

**Why it works:** Build-in-public numbers content performs well because it's unpolished and specific — ties directly to your LinkedIn post, so the same real story works across platforms with different edits.

---

## Filming notes
- Always disclose it's AI on-screen somewhere — it's your strongest hook, not something to hide.
- Real audio > any voiceover. If a real call goes badly, that's still usable — post it and own it ("still tuning her, here's an honest one").
- Post #1 and #5 first — they need the least editing and prove the product fastest.
